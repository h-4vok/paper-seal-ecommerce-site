# Archived artwork scenes

Scene templates in this directory are preserved for reference and are not available to the product
mockup selector. Scene 15 (`15-chalk-workshop`) was archived after its only artwork assignment,
Flower Bed, moved to scene 14 (`14-burgundy-library`). No other artwork used scene 15.

The scene metadata is retained in `manifest.json`. To bring an archived scene back into use, review
its templates and move it into the approved registry through the normal mockup review workflow.
