# Packaging and dispatch — pre-launch procedure

This procedure is for paid UK online orders of finished, unframed prints. It does not cover fair sales. Do not enable live checkout until the packaging, carrier, stock and legal checks below are complete.

## Prepare an order

1. Confirm payment and the order details in Snipcart. Check each artwork, size, quantity and delivery address against the order record.
2. Check the physical stock and the online sellable-stock record. Set aside the ordered prints and update the record after any manual or fair sale so the same unit is not sold twice.
3. Check the destination and parcel size against the currently approved InPost service. Use Royal Mail when InPost does not accept the destination or parcel. Do not offer locker collection until point selection is integrated and tested.
4. Check the studio closure calendar. If the stated 2–3 working-day dispatch target cannot be met, contact the customer before dispatch with the revised date and their options.

## Pack and send

1. Inspect each print for damage and confirm it matches the order. Protect its surface and corners and keep it flat.
2. Use the size-specific rigid packaging approved by a physical test. For a mixed order, use the tested configuration or split into more than one parcel. Do not fold or roll a print to fit a cheaper service.
3. Record the final packed weight and dimensions for each parcel. Buy the eligible tracked service using those measurements, attach the label, and retain proof of posting. A3 in a C3 457 × 324 mm envelope exceeds Royal Mail's 450 mm small-parcel length limit and must be priced as a medium parcel if that envelope is used.
4. Record the carrier and every tracking number against the Snipcart order. Send the customer the dispatch confirmation and all tracking numbers. Charge delivery once per order even if the studio splits it into multiple parcels.
5. Monitor exceptions. For damage, loss, failed delivery or a returned parcel, contact the customer and resolve the order under the published returns policy; a carrier claim does not limit consumer rights.

## Evidence required before live payment

- Approve actual protective packaging for 7 × 5, A4 and A3 prints. Pack and ship-test single-size and mixed orders, including multiple A3 prints; record weight, dimensions, capacity, condition on arrival and cost.
- Obtain carrier acceptance for the print and packaging, and verify current prices, eligible postcodes, compensation and collection/drop-off arrangements. Confirm the Northern Ireland process before offering it.
- Configure checkout methods and charges to match the tested packages and the public delivery page. Test 7 × 5, A4, A3, mixed baskets, the £44.99/£45 threshold after discounts, InPost-eligible and fallback postcodes, split parcels and order confirmations.
- Verify stock cannot be oversold, including the last unit in two simultaneous checkouts. Confirm cancellations, refunds, damaged stock and fair sales are reflected in the sellable-stock record.
- Confirm the legal business identity and geographic address, the final Terms and Privacy pages, support inboxes, and durable order-confirmation content before accepting payment.

The proprietor owns the physical packaging choice, carrier account and launch sign-off. Update this procedure and `/delivery` together when the tested method or price changes.
