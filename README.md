# Paperseal

Paperseal is an independent online storefront for a small British art boutique based around Eastbourne and East Sussex.

The shop will present and sell high-quality giclée art prints with a premium, warm, and distinctly local character. The experience should feel closer to a carefully curated gallery or art publication than to a generic ecommerce catalogue, while remaining straightforward and trustworthy for customers who simply want to browse, choose a print, and buy it.

## Project status

This repository is at the beginning of the project and does not have a technology stack yet. It currently contains project context and working agreements, but no application implementation.

The technical direction, framework, tooling, and deployment setup will be chosen as the project develops. Until then, documentation should describe intent and decisions without assuming a particular implementation.

## Product direction

The initial storefront is expected to support:

- browsing a catalogue of artworks;
- artwork detail pages with imagery, location, print specifications, pricing, and availability;
- fixed print-size variants and framed or unframed options;
- a mobile-friendly shopping flow;
- a custom storefront experience connected to Shopify for commerce and checkout.

The launch experience should prioritise visual quality, fast and discoverable pages, reliable purchasing, and low operational complexity. More immersive ideas—such as story-led discovery, recommendations, and illustrated geographic exploration—belong to the longer-term vision and should not block the initial launch.
